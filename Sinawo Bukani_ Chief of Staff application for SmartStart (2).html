My first 90 days

Built around what the role asks for. I'd take ownership of the CEO's follow-through from the first week, because that's what the role needs, and by day 90 I'd want the Office of the CEO to run on a rhythm that doesn't depend on me.

  1. Week 1

    Own the CEO's follow-through

    The role asks for: more CEO leverage and disciplined follow-through. I'd agree her top priorities for the quarter, run her follow-ups, route anything that isn't hers to decide to the right owner, and close open loops. I'd start a log of commitments and decisions with an owner and date for each, take over her Board and funder calendar, and begin SmartStart's child safeguarding induction.

  2. Days 8 to 30

    Run the executive rhythm and map decision rights

    The role asks for: executive governance and clear decision rights. I'd run the executive forum from week two, with an agenda, tracked actions and follow-up. I'd meet each executive and key partner, and map who decides what today and where decisions stall. On day 30, I'd give the CEO a written read-out: what I found, what I'd change first, and what I'd leave alone.

  3. Days 31 to 60

    Own the Board cycle and the risk picture

    The role asks for: Board enablement and legal and governance oversight. I'd run the Board and committee cycle: calendar, papers in a standard decision-ready format, and follow-up on resolutions. With specialist advisers, I'd build a governance and legal risk register so risks reach the CEO early and clearly. I'd send the CEO a monthly update on commitments, risks and decisions waiting.

  4. Days 61 to 90

    Close one process and scope expansion

    The role asks for: enterprise operations and expansion readiness. I'd take one operational process from problem to a working fix, handed to its permanent owner. I'd draft a scope for early expansion readiness, with market-entry decisions left to the CEO and Board. At day 90, I'd review with the CEO what is working, what I should hand over, and what comes next.
